Don't have the desktop app?

The itch.io app allows you to recieve Wolves updates as you would with a traditional launcher, otherwise you may have to download the full game every time there is an update.

  1. Download the itch.io app
  2. Run the installer and log into your account.
  3. Refresh this page to download the game!
